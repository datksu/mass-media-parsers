# massmedia-parsers
This repository contains parsers for online Russian media outlets such as RBC.ru, Gazeta.ru, Lenta.ru, News.ru and RT.ru

All the parsers were developed by me as a part of my undergraduate research project on the topic "The Role of Economic News on the Ruble Exchange Rate in Shaping Russians’ Perceptions of the Country’s Economic Situation"

The parsers resulted in a dataset of approximately 204,000 news articles.
